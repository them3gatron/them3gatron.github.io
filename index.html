02 // Profile

Follow the infrastructure. Read the operator.

The interesting part is rarely the malware alone. It is the system around it.

M3GATR0N is an independent security researcher focused on identifying and tracking threat actor infrastructure, documenting adversary tradecraft, and analysing autonomous or AI-driven offensive platforms.

Research begins with publicly accessible internet-facing infrastructure and follows the evidence: services, certificates, exposed files, operator tooling, campaign artefacts, code, and the relationships between them.

Evidence firstClaims stay attached to artefacts, logs, code, and repeatable technical observations.
Passive boundaryFindings originate from passive observation of publicly accessible infrastructure.
Defender utilityResearch is structured for threat intelligence, incident response, hunting, and infrastructure pivots.
Responsible releasePublication follows research completion and responsible disclosure obligations.
03 // Method

From signal to defensible intelligence.

A compact workflow for turning exposed infrastructure into evidence-backed research without losing the technical chain of custody.

PHASE_01

Discover

Identify anomalous internet-facing services, infrastructure clusters, exposed assets, and operational mistakes.

PHASE_02

Correlate

Connect certificates, host traits, code, network indicators, filenames, accounts, and campaign timelines.

PHASE_03

Validate

Separate confirmed artefacts from analytic inference, preserve uncertainty, and test competing explanations.

PHASE_04

Disclose

Protect victims, coordinate where needed, and publish technical detail that helps defenders act.
